### Timetable
Day 1: Combine ingredients, 4x stretch & folds, bulk ferment, shape, place on parchment lined baking sheet.

Preheat oven to 425ºF with a baking steel for heat retention, then lower to around 375ºF after pan goes in. Bake 30 min, rotating the pan halfway through the bake.


<!-- notes -->
### Notes
Initially made 18 rolls with natural leaven (180g) and this was very crowded for a domestic sheet pan. Gotta make big here, so, either add some additional flour, make fewer, and definitely press flat. 

12 rolls mostly work on a sheet.
Main substitutes are oil in place of butter and the addition of diastatic malt powder, which helps significantly with the rise.
